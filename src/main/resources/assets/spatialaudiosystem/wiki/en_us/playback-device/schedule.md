# ♪ Schedule

![](bws:spatialaudiosystem:wiki/screens/playback-device-schedule__en_us.png)

The editor for playing **several media in order** from a [Playback Device](../playback-device.md). Open it with the device's **♪ Schedule** button.

[[TOC]]

## How to open

1. Right-click the [Playback Device](../playback-device.md) to open it
2. Click **♪ Schedule** — the schedule opens beside the main screen
3. Your inventory stays reachable while it is open, so you can drag media straight into the rows

## Operation

| What you want to do | How |
|---|---|
| Add an entry | Click **+ Add Entry** |
| Assign audio | Drag a recording medium from your inventory into the **slot at the right of the row** |
| Change the play count | **Hover the count (×N) and use the mouse wheel** |
| Reorder | The **▲ / ▼** at the left of the row |
| Preview one row | The row's **▶** |
| Delete an entry | The row's **✕** — the medium is handed back to you |
| Play everything in order | **▶ Play All** |
| Stop | **■** |

> [!TIP]
> There are no + / − buttons for numbers. **Hovering the value and scrolling** is the shared Manta UI idiom.

> [!NOTE]
> Playing all repeats each entry for its **play count** before moving to the next one. Rows with no audio are skipped.

## While it plays

The row currently sounding is **outlined**, and the outline moves along as the sequence advances.

## Limits

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Entries | 6 max |
| Play count | 1 to 10 |
